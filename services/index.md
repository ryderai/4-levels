# Services: the four levels

4Levels exists at the intersection of performance and purpose: a sports advisory firm that enhances athletes' foundations, elevates their visibility, enlightens their perspective, and empowers them to build a legacy that outlasts the game.

### Level 1: Enhance (Your foundation)

Enhance is the foundation everything else stands on. It covers how an athlete trains, how they recover, and the daily habits that keep them available and improving, season after season.

- **Strength & conditioning:** Training that builds the strength and engine the game asks for.
- **Skill development:** Focused work on the parts of your game that move you to the next level.
- **Nutrition & recovery:** Fueling and recovery habits that keep you on the floor.
- **Discipline & daily habits:** The routines that carry you through a long season and a long career.

### Level 2: Elevate (Your visibility)

Elevate is about being seen the right way. It covers how an athlete shows up online and in the media, and how that visibility turns into NIL deals, endorsements and partnerships.

- **Branding & social media:** A clear personal brand and social channels that help instead of hurt.
- **NIL deals & endorsements:** Finding, weighing and closing name, image and likeness deals and endorsements.
- **Public relations & media:** Handling press, interviews and public moments.
- **Marketing & partnerships:** Connecting with brands that fit who you are, for the long run.

### Level 3: Enlighten (Your perspective)

Enlighten is the education side of the game. It covers how money works, who is worth knowing, and how to read an opportunity or a contract before saying yes to it.

- **Financial literacy:** How money works: budgeting, saving and what a paycheck really means.
- **Networking & relationships:** Building the relationships that open doors during and after your career.
- **Career decision guidance:** Clear thinking for the big calls: schools, teams, transfers and offers.
- **Understanding opportunities & contracts:** Knowing what is in front of you, and what it is worth, before you sign.

### Level 4: Empower (Your legacy)

Empower looks past the final buzzer. It covers ownership, building wealth, giving back to the community, and planning a life after basketball that the athlete chooses for themselves.

- **Business ownership:** Learning what it takes to own and build a business of your own.
- **Investing & wealth building:** Understanding how wealth is built, with connections to banking and private wealth management professionals.
- **Community impact & philanthropy:** Camps, community work and giving back where it counts.
- **Legacy & life after hoops:** A plan for what comes next, made before you need it.

## NIL strategy framework

1. **Valuation & player analysis:** Evaluate market value through performance data, analytics and scouting reports to set positioning and potential.
2. **Brand positioning:** Define identity, audience and market value to stand out in a crowded NIL landscape.
3. **Digital presence:** Strengthen social media, content strategy and personal branding to increase visibility and engagement.
4. **Market outreach:** Connect with brands, collectives and partners to create real NIL opportunities.
5. **Deal execution:** Structure, negotiate and secure NIL deals that maximize both short-term earnings and long-term value.
6. **Long-term strategy:** Align NIL opportunities with career goals, professional ambitions and success after basketball.
