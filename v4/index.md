# 4Levels Sports Advisory — design preview v4

Careers that outlast the game. Representation, development and life-after-hoops planning for basketball players: high school, college, the NBA and overseas.

This page is a design preview. The current site's full content is at https://4-levels.vercel.app/index.md

## The four levels
- **Enhance** — your foundation: strength & conditioning, skill development, nutrition & recovery, discipline & daily habits.
- **Elevate** — your visibility: branding & social media, NIL deals & endorsements, public relations & media, marketing & partnerships.
- **Enlighten** — your perspective: financial literacy, networking & relationships, career decision guidance, opportunities & contracts.
- **Empower** — your legacy: business ownership, investing & wealth building, community impact & philanthropy, legacy & life after hoops.
