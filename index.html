NIL strategy

Six steps to the right deal.

NIL means name, image and likeness: getting paid for who you are. This is how 4Levels approaches it.

  1. Valuation & player analysis

    Evaluate market value through performance data, analytics and scouting reports to set positioning and potential.

  2. Brand positioning

    Define identity, audience and market value to stand out in a crowded NIL landscape.

  3. Digital presence

    Strengthen social media, content strategy and personal branding to increase visibility and engagement.

  4. Market outreach

    Connect with brands, collectives and partners to create real NIL opportunities.

  5. Deal execution

    Structure, negotiate and secure NIL deals that maximize both short-term earnings and long-term value.

  6. Long-term strategy

    Align NIL opportunities with career goals, professional ambitions and success after basketball.
